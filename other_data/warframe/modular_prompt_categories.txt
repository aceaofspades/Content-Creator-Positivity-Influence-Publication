===SECTION:header===
You are analyzing either a YouTube video transcript from a gaming content creator or a reddit submission and comments. Your goal is to understand how this creator/author feels about the current state of the game they are discussing.

## Response Rules
- Only return values from the allowed options listed for each field.
- If a field's content is not discussed, too vague to assess, or has no clear answer, return `"unclear"` or `"NA"` as specified.
- Do not infer or assume sentiment that isn't clearly expressed.
- If a text field needs a value NOT in the provided list, return it as `"OTHER - [your description]"` (e.g., `"OTHER - podcast interview"`).
- **Sarcasm and intent:** Always grade sentiment based on the creator's *intended meaning*, not the literal words used. If a creator sarcastically says "great job devs, love this update," that is negative sentiment. If you are unsure whether something is sarcastic, note it in `meta.doubts_and_caveats`.

## Field Definitions and Allowed Values
===END===

===SECTION:core_fields===
### overall_state_of_game.score
The creator's overall sentiment on the current state of the game.
**Allowed values:** `-2`, `-1`, `0`, `1`, `2`, `"unclear"`
- -2 = Extremely negative (game is terrible, worst it's ever been)
- -1 = Negative (significant problems, not enjoyable)
-  0 = Neutral (mixed feelings, no strong opinion either way)
-  1 = Positive (game is in a good place, enjoying it)
-  2 = Extremely positive (game is fantastic, best it's ever been)

### overall_state_of_game.summary
A 1-2 sentence summary of the creator's take on the game's state.
**Allowed values:** Free text summary, or `"unclear"` if not discernible.

### sentiment_dimensions (all fields)
Each field represents the creator's sentiment on a specific aspect of the game.
**Allowed values for ALL sentiment_dimensions fields:** `-2`, `-1`, `0`, `1`, `2`, `"unclear"`

Fields:
- `is_the_game_fun_right_now` — Are they enjoying the moment-to-moment gameplay?
- `is_there_enough_content_to_do` — Do they feel there's sufficient content/activities?
- `is_the_game_respecting_player_time` — Do they feel the grind/time investment is fair?
- `are_the_developers_making_good_decisions` — Do they trust the developer's choices?
- `is_the_monetization_fair` — How do they feel about in-game purchases/pricing/monetization?
- `is_the_game_heading_in_a_good_direction` — Do they feel the game's trajectory is positive?
- `is_the_game_worth_playing` — Would they say the game is worth someone's time?
- `is_pvp_in_a_good_state` — How do they feel about Conclave and PvP modes?
- `is_pve_in_a_good_state` — How do they feel about PvE content/modes?
===END===

===SECTION:meta===
### meta.game_of_focus
What video game is the primary focus of this video? If the video covers multiple games, list the one with the most discussion time.
**Allowed values:** `"[name of video game]"`, `"none"`, `"unclear"`

### meta.video_summary
A 2-3 sentence factual summary of what this video covers. Focus on topics discussed, not the creator's opinions. (e.g., "The creator reviews the latest update, focusing on weapon balance changes and the new Warframe. They also briefly discuss upcoming roadmap announcements.")
**Allowed values:** Free text summary.

### meta.game_relevance
How much of this video is actually about the game's current state vs. other content (personal stories, other games, tangential topics)?
**Allowed values:** `"fully_relevant"`, `"mostly_relevant"`, `"partially_relevant"`, `"not_relevant"`

### meta.video_type_primary
The single best-fit category for this video.
**Allowed values:** `"rant"`, `"review"`, `"discussion"`, `"news"`, `"guide"`, `"build_guide"`, `"tier_list"`, `"farewell"`, `"return"`, `"reaction"`, `"podcast"`, `"stream_vod"`, `"montage"`, `"comedy"`, `"lore"`, `"OTHER - [description]"`

### meta.video_types_applicable
All categories that apply to this video. Return as an array.
**Allowed values:** Same as video_type_primary. Return one or more.

### meta.reasoning
A brief explanation of how you arrived at the `overall_state_of_game.score` and the key `sentiment_dimensions` scores (if applicable). Focus on the main signals you used from the transcript — what statements, topics, or tone drove your assessment. Do not rehash every field; summarize the 3-5 most important factors.
**Allowed values:** Free text, 2-4 sentences.

### meta.doubts_and_caveats
Flag anything that made you uncertain about your grading. Examples include: sarcasm you weren't fully confident about, transcript quality issues (garbled auto-captions, missing words), the creator being intentionally hyperbolic or performing a character, mixed signals where the creator contradicts themselves, or sections where context was missing.
**Allowed values:** Free text, or `"none"` if you have no concerns.

### meta.transcript_quality
How usable was the transcript for sentiment analysis?
**Allowed values:** `-2`, `-1`, `0`, `1`, `2`
- -2 = Completely unusable (transcript is too broken to extract meaningful sentiment; most sentiment fields should be `"unclear"`)
- -1 = Poor (significant issues — heavy garbling, large missing sections, speaker confusion — that affected grading accuracy)
-  0 = Acceptable (some minor issues like missing punctuation or occasional garbling, but meaning is clear)
-  1 = Good (clear, well-formatted, easy to follow)
-  2 = Excellent (clean, well-punctuated, no issues whatsoever)

### meta.confidence_score
How confident are you in the accuracy of your overall assessment? Consider the following factors: transcript quality, how clearly the creator expressed their opinions, the amount of sarcasm or hyperbole present, how much of the content was actually about the game's current state, and whether the creator's statements were consistent or contradictory.
**Allowed values:** `-2`, `-1`, `0`, `1`, `2`
- -2 = No confidence (major issues across multiple factors; assessment is essentially a guess)
- -1 = Low confidence (notable issues with one or more factors that likely affected accuracy)
-  0 = Moderate confidence (reasonable assessment but with meaningful uncertainty)
-  1 = High confidence (clear opinions, good transcript, minimal ambiguity)
-  2 = Full confidence (unambiguous opinions, clean transcript, straightforward content)

### meta.sarcasm_detected
Was sarcasm pervasive enough in this content that it could systematically affect sentiment scoring? Only flag this if sarcasm was a dominant communication style throughout, not for occasional sarcastic quips (which are normal in gaming content).
**Allowed values:** `true`, `false`

### meta.is_sponsored_by_game
Is this content sponsored or paid for by the game's developer or publisher? Only flag sponsorships/partnerships where the game being discussed is itself the sponsored product. Do NOT flag unrelated sponsorships (e.g., a GFuel ad read, a VPN sponsor, gaming peripheral promotions) — these are irrelevant to sentiment bias.
**Allowed values:** `true`, `false`, `"unclear"`
